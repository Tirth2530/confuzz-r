CONFUZZ-R ·

Failure hypergraph of the demo conference

Each dot on the ring is one atomic disruption. Each hub joins the disruptions of one minimal failing set: together they break the schedule, while every smaller subset passes. A ring around a single disruption means it now fails on its own.

Every element is computed by the deterministic simulator: minimal sets come from exhaustive enumeration of all 256 disruption combinations, and labels compare the patched schedule with the original. Regression: passed before the repair, fails after it. Residual: already failed before, newly minimal because a smaller failure was fixed. Subsumed: still fails, because a smaller set now fails. Fixed: now passes. Source data: experiments/results/hypergraph_demo.json.
